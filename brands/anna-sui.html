Founder story

Sui came to burned-out 1970s New York to meet Warhol and go to Max’s; she ended up at CBGB and the Mudd Club every night. She was designing sportswear at Glenora when friends making punk jewelry invited her to share a booth at the Boutique Show. Macy’s and Bloomingdale’s ordered a New Wave capsule; The New York Times ran a full-page ad; her boss fired her; she used the last paycheck to fund the first full collection.

In 1991 Madonna wore Sui to a Gaultier show in Paris; Meisel, Naomi, Linda and Christy pushed her onto a runway. Spring 1993 she did grunge the same season as Marc Jacobs at Perry Ellis — thrift, army surplus, Docs, stripes — then Spring 1994 babydoll dresses after watching Kurt Cobain wear vintage dresses onstage.

“When I started my own business, my main reason for designing clothes was that I wanted to dress rock stars and the people who went to rock concerts. It didn’t go beyond that aspiration at that point.”

Lisa Marie Presley, Interview
