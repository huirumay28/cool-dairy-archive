Founder story

They bonded over craft and traditional dress. Chopova’s research trips to Bulgaria turned discarded village aprons and dowry textiles — once made on household looms for brides — into patchwork. Lowena climbs; the hardware is mountaineering carabiners on a deadstock-leather belt. The carabiner skirt was the MA piece. MatchesFashion took 30 skirts and they sold out.

They have said they want Kathleen Hanna in one. First scheduled runway, Sept 2022, mixed metal-kid energy with lacrosse and folklore. Emma on the punk read: they didn’t start with it in mind; the first MA collection was about rock climbing; through the combination of things, it started to look very punk.

“We’ve both always loved wearing fun skirts, so it made sense to create one together. It’s important to us to sustainably source materials, as well.”

Emma Chopova, British Vogue

“Our childhood favourites like Kathleen Hanna, and Bianca Casady of CocoRosie, but Billie Eilish would be incredibly cool, as well.”

Laura Lowena, on who should wear the carabiner skirt, British Vogue

“I think it was something that we always liked, but we didn’t start with it in mind. It was because our first collection out of the MA was about rock climbing… through the combination of things, it did start to look very punk. Maybe it was a subconscious thing.”

Emma Chopova, Document Journal · 2019
