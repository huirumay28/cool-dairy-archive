Founder story

Kitamura was a Shinjuku record kid (Sex Pistols, Blondie, new wave) who saw a Tokyo Mode Gakuen ad and enrolled. He rejected the CDG/Yohji minimalism conquering the West and built a Harajuku line first for teen girls, then unisex: American 60s–70s pop, porn, comics, cars, rock tees. Olive Magazine made the graphics famous. Before Bape or Undercover, this was the rebellious Harajuku tee.

He named the mood after Patti Smith’s stage energy and Debbie Harry’s glamour; he made a Sonic Youth tee for Kim Gordon; Kurt Cobain wore Hysteric; Gwen name-checked the brand in Harajuku Girls. Do not invent a woman founder.
