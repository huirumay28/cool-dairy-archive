Vivienne Westwood

Vivienne Westwood (designer) + Malcolm McLaren (partner, later Sex Pistols manager). McLaren was the shop/idea partner; Westwood cut and made the clothes.

Founder story

Westwood and McLaren were bored of late-60s hippie London and obsessed with 1950s rebellion. In 1971 McLaren, dressed in a powder-blue teddy-boy suit, ducked into a King’s Road phone box in the rain; a shop owner across the street at 430 invited him in, then vanished and left him the keys. Westwood started knocking up Teddy Boy clothes, then biker leather, then rubber.

In 1974 they bolted huge pink padded letters spelling SEX onto the facade. December 1976 they reopened as Seditionaries: Clothes for Heroes — bondage straps, tartan, zips. After the Pistols collapsed they refitted the shop as World’s End and in 1981 showed Pirate, Westwood’s first official catwalk collection.

“It changed the way people looked. I was messianic about punk, seeing if one could put a spoke in the system in some way. I realised there was no subversion without ideas. It’s not enough to want to destroy everything.”

The Independent · 2002

“If you invest in art, if you study yourself, you become a freedom fighter immediately because your life changes, you get off the consumer treadmill, and you start thinking. My thing as a fashion designer [is] I said buy less clothes. Keep wearing things that you’ve really chosen that you really love. That is status. It’s not that you have to keep consuming. A status symbol is a book.”

The Guardian (video, 25 April 2011) · 2011

Style codes

  • Bondage trousers with hobble straps and towelling bum-flap
  • Parachute shirts, tartan, safety-pin and slogan tees
  • Later Mini-Crini crinolines and Harris Tweed dandy suiting
  • Attitude: confrontational, romantic-primitive, clothes as protest

Signature pieces

  • Seditionaries bondage suit, S/S 1976 — Black cotton, zips, D-rings, hobble straps. V&A T.252&A-1989; Met 2004.15a–c.
  • Pirate collection, 1981 — Gold/orange romantic pirate, first official Westwood/McLaren catwalk.
