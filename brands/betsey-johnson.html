Founder story

Johnson won the Mademoiselle guest-editor contest and walked into Paul Young’s Paraphernalia, the shop that tried to import Swinging London to Manhattan. Edie Sedgwick was her house model. In 1969 Johnson, Bunky Washburn and Nini Latorre opened Betsey Bunky Nini on East 53rd.

The 1978 own-label years, then the 80s “Punk” labels, are the Cool Dairy years: stretch, acid color, heart logos, a cartwheel at the end of every show.
