Comme des Garçons

Rei Kawakubo. CEO Adrian Joffe is her husband; she remains the designer.

Founded Label 1969, Tokyo; company 1973; Paris debut 1981 · Women-founded: yes. · filed Sunday 16 August 2026

Founder story

Her mother left her father when he would not let her work. Kawakubo left home after college, styled shoots, then designed clothes because Japanese shops did not have what she needed. The name is from Françoise Hardy’s song Tous les garçons et les filles — clothes like the boys, for women who do not dress to seduce.

Paris 1981–82 was a scandal: black, holes, unfinished seams. WWD called the 1982 Destroy collection the Hiroshima bag lady look — a critic insult, not her slogan. She has said she felt an affinity with the punk spirit: punk is against flattery, and that is what she likes about it. The 2017 Met show Art of the In-Between put her in the same museum conversation as Westwood.

“I’ve always felt an affinity with the punk spirit. I like that word. Every collection is that. Punk is against flattery, and that’s what I like about it.”

System magazine, issue 2 (Adrian Joffe translating)

Style codes

  • Black, asymmetry, holes, volumes that ignore the waist
  • Later sculptural white and bows
  • Attitude: anti-fashion, start from zero — the last is a common paraphrase; the sourced line is punk against flattery
