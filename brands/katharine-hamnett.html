Katharine Hamnett

Founder story

Saint Martins, then a freelance decade, then her own label in 1979. In 1983 she printed giant block slogans on oversized tees — CHOOSE LIFE, EDUCATION NOT MISSILES, WORLDWIDE NUCLEAR BAN NOW — designed to be copied. Wham! wore CHOOSE LIFE in “Wake Me Up Before You Go-Go”; Roger Taylor wore WORLDWIDE NUCLEAR BAN NOW at Rock in Rio.

The anecdote that still works: 1984, Downing Street, she met Margaret Thatcher wearing 58% DON’T WANT PERSHING and made the front pages. She has spent decades clawing CHOOSE LIFE back from the US anti-abortion lobby.

“I was having an argument with Lynne Franks… I said, ‘this is going to have absolutely no effect, you’d be better off printing the central Buddhist tenet, which is “Choose Life”, in giant letters on a white t-shirt’. So I went and did it - I just thought, why not?”

The Telegraph · 2018

“The US anti-abortion lobby attempted to appropriate CHOOSE LIFE. We are taking it back and promoting its real meaning. Ours is authentic and I believe in a woman’s right to choose.”
