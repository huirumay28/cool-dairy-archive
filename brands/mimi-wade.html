Founder story

Added 26 Oct 2026 digest. Her CSM graduate collection, Debutante Drunk (2015), was hand-painted leather dresses with matching opera gloves carrying horror-movie graphics, plus torn, hand-dyed lace-trimmed slips. Tutors told her anyone could buy those slips from Carine Gilson or Agent Provocateur. She went back and ripped the lace, mixed the colours and cut the hems until they looked like her. The first time she saw them in print was on Lana Del Rey in Marfa Journal and Pamela Anderson in Playboy, shot by Ellen von Unwerth.

Her muse is her grandmother, actress Pamela Curran, “Granny Pammy”: a Life cover debutante at 17 in 1947, divorced a year later, a teenage single mother who became an actress against her family’s wishes and appeared in The Blob and I Dream of Jeannie. Wade calls her “quite punk in her attitude, even though she doesn’t dress like a generic punk.” Her mother, Rebecca Wade, studied at Saint Martins and was an Eighties stylist.

The work is both love letter and critique. After the Weinstein revelations she said her granny’s stories from Hollywood in the 1950s to early 1970s felt more relevant than ever. One season’s muse was Daphne du Maurier, with prints about her being boxed into “gothic romance” because of her gender.

“Although I'm continually inspired and fascinated by Old Hollywood, that's not to say that it isn't problematic. My work is both an appreciation and a critique.”
