Ann Demeulemeester

Ann Demeulemeester (born Ann Verhelst, 1959). Current creative director is Stefano Gallici — say so; the vintage that matters is hers, 1987–2013.

Founded 1985, Antwerp, with Patrick Robyn. First Paris show 1991. She left the house in 2013. · Women-founded: yes. · filed Sunday 16 August 2026

Founder story

She is Antwerp Six. Pregnant and unable to travel to the 1986 London group show, she sent sunglasses; Barneys still ordered. The real origin is a record-shop window: at sixteen she saw Patti Smith’s Horses — white shirt, cuffs cut off, black ribbon. Years later she mailed Smith three white shirts she had made; Smith, then living quietly in Detroit, never forgot.

Smith has said that since she started performing again she has never gone onstage without wearing Ann. One S/S 1998 jacket became a Patti favorite, worn to death, and is now in the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame. PJ Harvey’s 2011 belt is the other end of that house.

“I saw this record Horses and I didn’t know anything about it, but I went inside and bought it and put it on my turntable and as I listened, I thought, I have to know this person. I will know this person one day.”

Vogue, with Patti Smith · 2014

“You can call it a corset, but it’s not, it’s a big belt I made… What I wanted to do with this was exactly the opposite of a corset… I wanted to make a shield or a kind of armor that would protect the fragility of a feminine body inside.”

Vogue, Pitti Uomo retrospective, on the PJ Harvey belt
