BodyMap

Stevie Stewart + David Holah (both Middlesex Polytechnic, 1979–82). Women-co-founded: yes. Not women-only — say the partnership clearly.

Founder story

They met day one at Middlesex. Graduation collection bought by Browns. The name comes from artist Enrico Job, who photographed every part of his body and collaged a flat “body map” — they cut patterns the same way, holes in unexpected places, flesh as part of the garment.

Collection titles were feral: Barbie Takes a Trip, Querelle Meets Olive Oil, The Cat in the Hat Takes a Rumble with the Techno Fish. They rebranded lycra as high fashion, dressed Michael Clark’s dancers, and were the house look of 80s London clubland. After a New York “New London” show produced with Susanne Bartsch they were stars overnight, then the business imploded.
