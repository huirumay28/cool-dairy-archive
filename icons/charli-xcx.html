Charli XCX

A pop star who dresses like she would in real life, and buys a lot of it second-hand.

Born 1992, Cambridge, England · 2008– · filed Sunday 16 August 2026

The person

English singer and songwriter. Added in the 28 Oct 2026 pulse because of her Vinted announcement: a tour wardrobe built partly from second-hand finds, and a resale of her own closet for charity.

The work

Her Music, Fashion, Film tour was a 12-date North American arena run that ended in Las Vegas on 23 October 2026, with guests including Chappell Roan, Tinashe and Underscores (ELLE UK).

Why the look existed

On this tour she and her longtime stylist Chris Horan aimed for clothes she would wear in real life rather than dressing like a pop star (ELLE UK), and sourced pre-loved pieces on Vinted with search terms like “distressed”, “gothic” and “flounce”.

“Vintage and second-hand are a huge part of my style. Chris and I love mixing older, iconic pieces with newer things. I think it makes a look feel more personal to me and always takes us to a more unexpected place.”

Vinted newsroom announcement · 2026

“Some were found on Vinted in the first place, worn on stage, and are now going back out into the world. Which feels like a full circle.”

Vinted newsroom announcement · 2026

2026

Music, Fashion, Film tour — second-hand on stage, then back out to resale (added 28 Oct 2026 pulse)

A headline pop tour dressed partly from second-hand listings, with the clothes sent back into circulation afterwards. It is the thrift loop cool dairy runs on, at arena scale.

Garments
Tour pieces sourced on Vinted: an early-2000s Christian Audigier piece reworked into a dress for Austin City Limits, a sheer grey blouse worn in San Diego, and a black Roberto Cavalli dress worn to the Academy Gala after-party in Los Angeles and at the Las Vegas finale. Announced for her Vinted resale: ripped-up Ann Demeulemeester tees, Balmain lace-up leather trousers and a brocade corset bodysuit worn on tour, sheer Betsey Johnson tops worn on stage, Zana Bayne lace-and-leather pieces from the “Rock Music” video, an R&M Leathers leather jacket, a pink Acne Studios dress, a custom silver Zadig & Voltaire blazer and vintage tees.
Designer / season
Labels as named by Vinted, ELLE UK and NYLON. No season is given for any piece, and the Audigier is dated only “early-2000s”.
Where it lives
Selected pieces go on sale on Vinted in the UK, US and Australia from Monday 16 November 2026. All proceeds go to Rainbow Railroad, which helps LGBTQI+ people facing persecution reach safety.

Airbnb.org gala — archive Roberto Cavalli on the red carpet (added 29 Oct 2026 pulse)

Days after the Vinted announcement, she wears a twenty-year-old Cavalli runway gown to a charity gala instead of a new-season loan. Archive dressing on a red carpet is the cool dairy argument in one picture.

Garments
A strapless bronze-gold lamé gown, ruched through the bodice and hips, with black floral appliqué down the front, a high front slit edged in black feathers and a black feather hem. Worn with black satin pumps at Airbnb.org's first gala, Milk Studios, Los Angeles, 28 October 2026.
Designer / season
Roberto Cavalli, per Just Jared's red-carpet gallery. The Fall/Winter 2005 season is a fan identification (X user @ship_gq), not confirmed by press; it lines up visually with a Cavalli runway look and with Vogue's Fall 2005 review, which describes lamé and feathered eveningwear.
Where it lives
Worn on the red carpet; no resale or Vinted link announced for this piece.
