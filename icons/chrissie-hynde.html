Chrissie Hynde

Leather, eyeliner and a refusal to dress sexy: a shield, not a costume.

Born 1951, Akron, Ohio · The Pretenders 1978– · filed Sunday 16 August 2026

The person

Born in Akron, Ohio in 1951. She studied art at Kent State during the Vietnam-era student protests, dropped out in 1973 and moved to London. There she wrote for the NME and worked on the shop floor at Vivienne Westwood and Malcolm McLaren’s SEX on the King’s Road, outfitting the punk scene before she had a band of her own.

She tried to marry first Johnny Rotten and then Sid Vicious to get a work permit. Neither happened. In 1978 she met the musicians who became the Pretenders. (Added 26 Oct 2026 digest.)

The work

The Pretenders’ 1979 debut went to No. 1 in the UK; “Brass in Pocket” was the first new UK No. 1 of the 1980s. On the cover she scowls straight at the camera in a zipped red leather motorcycle jacket and fingerless black lace gloves, while the men beside her do new wave detachment.

Why the look existed

Skin-tight but shows little skin. Black leather trousers, a red leather biker, heavy black eyeliner under a curtain of fringe: tough enough that nobody mistakes her for the girl singer.

“I don’t feel quite like a woman until I’ve got my eyes drawn on.”

Rolling Stone, “The Pretenders Ain’t Sobbing” · 1980

“I don’t try to be tacky and out of fashion, but somehow I can never get it right. If I put on something pretty, it would be a joke, you know? I can’t help it.”

Rolling Stone · 1980

“The idea of trying to be sexy was repellant to me. Something I’d never deliberately do.”

Reckless (memoir), quoted by i-D

“There isn’t enough ‘f*ck off’ in music today. And there’s too much attention paid to fashion… Most of the time I just look like a roadie.”

Pretenders debut — red leather biker and lace gloves (added 26 Oct 2026 digest)

A former SEX shop girl who took the punk uniform into the charts without the safety pins. She refused to dress sexy after a bad outfit incident with a rubber skirt: “Over-the-knee boots was the limit.”

Garments
Zipped red leather motorcycle jacket (“It’s got my perfume in it”), black leather trousers, dark denim, fingerless black lace gloves, Dusty Springfield-style black eyeliner, long fringe. Off-duty: a screaming-pink fake-fur coonskin cap bought in London. On the first tour she sometimes played in a T-shirt printed “bitch”.
