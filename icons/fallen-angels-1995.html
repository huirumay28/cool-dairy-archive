Fallen Angels 墮落天使 (1995)

William Chang’s 90s Hong Kong cool: East and West worn whenever you want.

Era file · 90s Hong Kong · filed Sunday 16 August 2026

The person

Fallen Angels is the sister film to Chungking Express (see the Faye Wong file). William Chang did the costumes and sets and was nominated at the Hong Kong Film Awards for Best Costume and Make-up Design. Karen Mok won Best Supporting Actress.

The work

“You can use the Western side of you, or you can use Eastern side, whenever you want. It’s fluid.”

William Chang, WestEast · 2005

“That dress is all about restraint.”

William Chang on the qipao, WestEast · 2005
