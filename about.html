About

The paper next to the rail

Cool Dairy is Huiru Huang’s vintage shop. This is the archive that sits beside it — research, not merch.

Sunday 16 August 2026

Cave Daily is the other desk: long, quiet, magazine-shaped. This file is newsprint. Milk-glass white, black ink, and one hot-pink accent — the unsigned 1995 neoprene catsuit now V&A S.158-2021, used here as a colour, not as a throne.

We are not a startup landing page and we are not selling a lifestyle. We are filing people and looks so the shop can buy, refuse, and talk without inventing a house.

How a page is written

Icon pages open with the person — who she is, what she made, what she said — and only then the clothes. Brand pages open with the garments, then the founder story. Every look names the garment, then designer and season only if verified, then where the piece lives now, then sources as real links. If a photograph is not on this disk, the page gives a photo-pair: a search string and a public URL. We do not hotlink rights-locked covers. We do not invent YouTube IDs; unverified clips stay as links.

How to add a file

Drop a JSON into content/icons/ or content/brands/ and run python3 generate.py. The homepage grids read the folders. There is no hardcoded list of names to edit.

Hard no’s, already decided

No invented designer on the 1995 pink catsuit. No invented designer on the Down by the Water dress — it is a TK Maxx nightgown. No Prada on Fiona Apple’s 1997 VMA crochet. No season on Christian Joy, Annie Mochnacz, Todd Lynn tour pieces, or Stephen Sprouse 1978. No Olivia × Balenciaga SS06 Lollapalooza. Do not use the 2007 lyrics dress as Let England Shake, or the 2016 Primavera shot as White Chalk.

Open the HTML files in a browser after generate.py runs. Sources on every page. We don’t invent designers or quotes.
